Who we are

The plumbing behind modern money products.

We build the infrastructure that lets any team — anywhere — ship financial products without rebuilding the same brittle stack from scratch.

Since 2019

One frustrated engineer turned into 240 people across four hubs.

Stackly started because our founder spent a year building the same ledger, compliance stack and reconciliation pipeline at three different fintechs. The conclusion was unavoidable: the plumbing was the product, and nobody should have to build it twice.

Today we process billions annually across 40+ markets — still the same company at heart: small teams, high ownership, obsessive about correctness.
